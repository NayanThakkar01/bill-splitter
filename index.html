Exact Subunit Balancing Logic

Why do standard calculators lose cents / paise?

When dividing $100.00 amongst 3 people, each person theoretically owes $33.3333... If rounded normally to $33.33, three people pay $99.99, leaving a 1-cent shortfall.

How HisaabKitab solves this:

HisaabKitab converts the entire bill to exact integer subunits (e.g. 10,000 cents or paise). The remainder subunits (totalSubunits % numPeople) are allocated sequentially (+0.01 to the first person) so that:

Person 1: 33.34 + Person 2: 33.33 + Person 3: 33.33 = 100.00 exactly.

This mathematically guarantees an exact 0.00 discrepancy on every single split across all supported currencies.
